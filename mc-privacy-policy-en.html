Privacy Policy — Master: The Chef Jury

Türkçe · Terms of Service

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Master: The Chef Jury (package me.audicu707.masterthejury, the “App”) handles information. The App is developed by audicu707 (“we”, “us”).

Short version

  • We do not collect personal data ourselves. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and your game progress stays on your device.
  • The App shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google collects certain device and usage information, described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

To let you keep playing, the App saves game data locally on your device only using the device’s standard storage: the season in progress, your career (finished seasons, champions, endings, achievements, the dishes in your cookbook), your settings (language and colour theme) and whether you bought “Remove Ads”. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

The scores, critiques and choices you make in the game, including any critique you type yourself, are game moves. They stay on your device and are not collected, transmitted or used for advertising.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), a service of Google LLC, to display full-screen ads between episodes. When an ad is requested or shown, the SDK may automatically collect and share with Google information such as:

  • Device identifiers — the Android Advertising ID and similar identifiers;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country or city level);
  • Device and app information — device model, operating-system version, language, app version;
  • Ad interactions — which ads were shown, taps and views, and related app-activity signals;
  • Diagnostics — performance and crash data relating to the ad SDK.

Google uses this information to deliver and personalise ads (where you have allowed it), to measure ad performance, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not receive your Advertising ID or any other data that identifies you personally from Google; we only receive aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

When you set up a new season, the App may also show its own message offering the in-app “Remove Ads” purchase. This offer is part of the App and collects no data.

Connection check

Because the App is funded by ads, it needs an internet connection unless you bought “Remove Ads”. To know whether you are online, the App reads the connection status that your device’s operating system already provides. It does not send any request of its own for this, and no information leaves your device because of it.

In-app purchase (“Remove Ads”)

The App offers an optional one-time purchase that removes ads. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy policy; we never see or store your payment details. The App only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is owned and saves an “ads removed” flag on your device, so the App stays ad-free offline and after a reinstall (via Settings → Restore purchase).

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch the App shows a consent form provided by Google. You can accept or refuse personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in the App under Settings → Ad privacy options. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads, which rely on contextual information rather than your profile.
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through the same privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Remove Ads: with the one-time purchase, the App no longer requests or shows ads.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all collection by the App and removes all locally stored game data.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. The information described in the Advertising section is collected and processed by Google as our advertising provider. We do not share any other data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device ad settings. Local game data is removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit (HTTPS).

Children

The App is a cooking-contest judging game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that caused data to be collected, contact us and we will help.

Fiction

All contestants, judges, the channel and the show in the App are fictional. Any resemblance to real people or contests is coincidental.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
